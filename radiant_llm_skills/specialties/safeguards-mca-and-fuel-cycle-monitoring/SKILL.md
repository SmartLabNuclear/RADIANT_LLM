# Safeguards, MC&A, and Fuel-Cycle Monitoring Skill

## Purpose
Support structured discussion of safeguards, material control and accounting, diversion pathways, containment and surveillance, and monitoring challenges in advanced fuel cycles.

## When To Use
- Safeguards, MC&A, inventory difference, diversion, containment and surveillance, verification, molten salt fuel cycles, online processing, or process monitoring questions

## Core Questions This Skill Helps Answer
- What safeguards challenge is being discussed?
- Which observables, inventories, or monitoring points matter?
- What makes advanced fuel cycles harder to verify than traditional solid-fuel systems?

## Scope and Non-Goals
- This skill supports technical interpretation of safeguards concepts.
- It does not provide legal determinations, inspection authority, or facility-specific classified safeguards design.

## Required Evidence Posture
- Distinguish safeguards objectives from safety and security objectives while noting interactions.
- When discussing inventories or measurement uncertainty, state the accounting basis and assumptions.
- Avoid claiming detectability or verification sufficiency without an explicit monitoring architecture.

## Reasoning Checklist
- Define the material form, process state, measurement point, and monitoring cadence.
- Separate accountancy, containment/surveillance, and process-monitoring arguments.
- Note whether the reactor or fuel-cycle concept introduces online movement or transformation of material.

## Preferred Source Hierarchy
1. This skill's reference anchors and equation/formula notes
2. User local RAG evidence
3. Stable safeguards and fuel-cycle monitoring literature

## References
- IAEA safeguards guidance and introductory safeguards references: use for safeguards objectives, verification language, and containment/surveillance concepts.
- Advanced fuel-cycle safeguards literature for molten salt reactors and continuously processed systems: use for online inventory, process-monitoring challenges, and measurement-point discussions.
- MC&A and process-monitoring references: use for inventory difference, material balance, uncertainty, and observability framing.

## Equations and Formulae
- Material balance identity: beginning inventory + additions - removals - ending inventory = inventory difference. Use only when boundaries, time period, and material categorization are defined.
- Uncertainty propagation: discuss combined uncertainty only when contributing measurements and covariance assumptions are known. Do not fabricate numerical sigma thresholds or alarm criteria.
- Process monitoring: for online or liquid-fuel systems, state whether the argument is based on direct assay, inferred state estimation, or process-monitoring proxies.

## Examples
- Explain why online-processed or liquid-fuel systems complicate traditional material accountancy.
- Draft a safeguards-oriented comparison between direct measurement and process-monitoring approaches.
- Summarize a material-balance discussion while keeping uncertainties and assumptions explicit.

## Limitations and Disclaimer
- Applicability depends strongly on fuel form, process configuration, and governing safeguards regime.
- Verify with current safeguards authorities, facility data, and qualified safeguards specialists before using content for program decisions.
